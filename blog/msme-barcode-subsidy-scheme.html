Pricing

The MSME barcode subsidy most businesses never claim

Up to 80% off your GS1 registration fees — if you know it exists and qualify as a micro-manufacturer.

UPDATED JUL 20265 MIN READ

Most GS1 registration guides skip this entirely: if you qualify as a micro-manufacturing enterprise, the government will reimburse a large chunk of what you pay GS1 India — not through GS1 itself, but through a separate MSME scheme.

In short: Micro-manufacturing enterprises can get up to 80% reimbursement on both the one-time registration fee and annual recurring fees, capped at ₹50,650, for either 100 or 1,000 barcodes.

Who actually qualifies

The scheme is scoped narrowly on purpose — it's for micro-manufacturing enterprises, not every small business. If you're a trader, reseller, or service business rather than a manufacturer, this particular subsidy doesn't apply to you, even if you're otherwise classified as an MSME. Manufacturing your own product (private-label counts) is the key qualifying condition.

What's actually covered

  • The one-time GS1 registration fee
  • Annual recurring fees — for the first 3 years
  • Available at two barcode-volume tiers: 100 barcodes or 1,000 barcodes

The reimbursement rate is up to 80% of eligible fees, subject to the overall cap of ₹50,650. In practice this means the scheme is most valuable for businesses at the smaller end of the MSME spectrum, where 80% of a modest registration fee makes a real dent in the actual out-of-pocket cost.

Why most businesses never claim it

Two reasons show up repeatedly: businesses register for GS1 barcodes without ever checking MSME scheme eligibility first (the two processes aren't automatically linked), and the paperwork for the subsidy is separate from the GS1 registration paperwork — so it's an easy step to simply not know about.

How to actually claim it

The subsidy isn't processed by GS1 India directly — it runs through India's MSME support channels. Practically, that means:

  • Confirm your Udyam (MSME) registration is active and correctly classifies you as a micro-manufacturing enterprise before applying for your GS1 barcode
  • Keep your GS1 registration invoice and payment proof — you'll need it as supporting documentation for the reimbursement claim
  • Apply for reimbursement through the applicable MSME scheme channel rather than assuming GS1 India applies any discount automatically at checkout
Scheme terms, caps, and application channels are periodically revised — confirm current details with your MSME/Udyam registration authority before budgeting around this subsidy.
