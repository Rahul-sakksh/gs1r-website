How to register a GS1 barcode in India — step by step

Every step from checking eligibility to receiving your GTINs, with the documents and fees you'll actually need at each stage.

UPDATED JUL 2026 8 MIN READ

If you sell a physical product in India — through retail, modern trade, or e-commerce marketplaces like Amazon and Flipkart — you'll eventually be asked for a barcode that starts with a GS1-issued number. This guide walks through registration itself, without the detours.

In short: you apply through GS1 India, pay a fee based on your business turnover and how many barcodes you need, and receive a company prefix plus a set of GTINs you assign to your products.

Who needs to register

Any business that manufactures, packages, or private-labels a product intended for retail sale generally needs its own GS1 company prefix — you can't reuse another company's barcode range, even if a supplier already has one. This applies whether you sell through physical stores, your own website, or third-party marketplaces.

What you'll need before you start

  • Business registration proof (GST certificate, incorporation certificate, or partnership deed)
  • PAN of the business entity
  • A list of the products you intend to barcode (rough count is fine at this stage)
  • Your most recent annual turnover figure — this determines your fee slab

The registration process

1

Apply to GS1 India

Submit your business and turnover details along with the required documents through GS1 India's registration process.

2

Pay the applicable fee

Fees are tiered by turnover and number of barcodes required — see the table below for current slabs.

3

Receive your company prefix

GS1 India issues a unique prefix tied to your business — this is the root of every barcode you'll generate.

4

Generate your GTINs

Combine your prefix with a product reference number to create a distinct GTIN for each product variant (size, flavor, or SKU counts as a separate variant).

5

Produce and print barcodes

Convert each GTIN into a scannable barcode image and place it on packaging, meeting minimum size and contrast requirements for reliable scanning.

Typical fee ranges

Exact fees change periodically — always confirm current numbers directly with GS1 India before budgeting. As a general shape, expect registration fees to scale with turnover and renewal fees to recur annually:

After you're registered

Once your GTINs are live, your product can be listed, scanned, and tracked using a globally recognized identity. That solves discoverability and compliance — it doesn't, on its own, stop someone from copying that barcode onto a counterfeit product.
